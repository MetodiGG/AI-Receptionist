SHORT BEFORE CRITIQUE
---------------------
The baseline hid all category photographs behind a small CTA and required
separate picker steps. Equal low-contrast cream surfaces and small thumbnails
gave little visual hierarchy. The initial discovery area felt empty, while
step navigation obscured the relationship between a selection and its results.

- Search, always-visible 14-category grid, inline child services, summary, results.
- No "Избери услуга", step header/progress, picker stages or Back-between-steps.
- No category preselected on a fresh visit. Explicit valid links still restore.
- All categories render: 2 mobile / 3 tablet / 4 desktop columns. No carousel,
  hidden More list, new taxonomy identifiers, or page-only filter copy.

  matching, distance wording, URL restoration and Back/Forward remain intact.

  is an exact final public count.

  independently selectable nearest-only sort. There is no page-only sorting.

VISUAL DESIGN
-------------
- New scoped --inline-* and --premium-card-* tint/shade tokens only. Existing
  generic/search tokens and font family are unchanged.
- Static orange/peach radial mesh over warm cream; warm brown dark equivalents.
- Semi-opaque 89–94% cream/warm-brown panels with 16px backdrop blur, solid
  fallback, gradient fine edge, inner top highlight and two warm shadow layers.
- Radii: panel 26px (24 mobile), tiles 18px (16 mobile), photo 16px (14 mobile),
  chips 999px. No new decorative assets, neon, animated gradients or parallax.
- Photographic frames: 64px mobile; 80px desktop category, 72px service. Actual
  image interiors measure 62/78/70px because of the frame border.
- Full centered labels, 15–16px normal labels; natural whole-word wrapping.
  Desktop category names center in the remaining text area without an arrow.
  Photo-forward child tiles and no-photo All tile center across the full tile.
  The All tile's measured center delta was 0px.
- Selected tiles use tinted fill, an accent border/inner edge, bolder label and
  aria-pressed; not an underline or a checkmark.
- Pointer hover: 1px lift and photo scale1.04 inside its frame. Pressed scale.985.
  Actual browser hover matrices were measured; focus outline was 2px with offset.
- One espresso contrast language for the measured-safe sticky summary and parent
  chip. The summary only sticks if height <= min(240px,30% viewport), otherwise
  remains in normal flow. Its measured height+24px drives result scroll offsets.
- On 390px, the pinned summary starts at8px and the first card below it at179px;
  there is no overlay over the first business.
- A persistent presentation-only grid presence wrapper animates natural panel
  height, opacity and8px offset for200ms. Logical selection/history/requests are
  never delayed. Closed content is immediately inert and aria-hidden, retained
  only for the exit, then removed. Rapid reopen cancels cleanup. Reduced motion
  disposes immediately and disables transitions. Tile stagger ends within240ms.
- Card surfaces, two service/price chips, secondary directions, tertiary details
  and orange-gradient booking actions share the warm geometry/depth system.
- Homepage booking artwork now uses existing vector Icon(calendar), matched22px
  to Icon(map). The ordinary non-home BookingButtonContent artwork is preserved.
  The rest of the card source is exactly baseline, including labels, links,
  analytics, booking eligibility, external-site/phone/native distinctions.
