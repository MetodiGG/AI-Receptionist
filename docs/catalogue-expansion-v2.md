## Precise location layer and its limits

**122 records are located: 84 independently published owner points and 38 reviewed OSM address points.** The remaining 429 premises lack an approved point; 44 businesses are service areas, not offices. Unlocated premises remain usable in city searches but cannot honestly appear with invented km or be represented as within a 2 km circle. Car-wash and home-visit coverage in particular must not be confused with precise nearby coverage: this snapshot has no verified car-wash points, and home-visit areas intentionally have no office point. The new 1,500–2,000 count alone will not solve these gaps. The additional lawyer's and Game Castle points are explicitly printed by the owners; they did not require another geocoding request.

The owner approved one small one-time public-address geocoding batch. It completed **225 sequential Nominatim requests**, with start times spaced at least 1.1 seconds, one identifying process on one machine, a durable response cache, and no retries after provider errors. Only exact house/street/Sofia/country matches with a bounded building/address point were accepted. Street-only hits, district/city centroids, ranged/block-only addresses, ambiguous building suffixes and conflicting matches were rejected. User coordinates were never submitted. **Do not rerun it or schedule it under the completed approval.** The public service is not an unlimited data source, POI collector, autocomplete or runtime dependency; see the [Nominatim policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/).

- Reproducible offline cache: `docs/catalogue-geocoding/nominatim-address-cache-20261106.json`.
- Accepted, independently licensed address layer: `lib/discovery/catalogue-data/geocodes-v2.json`.
- Public machine-readable ODbL copy: `/catalogue-location-data-20261106.json`, linked alongside attribution in the UI. It includes only the 38 OSM-derived address points, not GoRez-owned partner records, service menus, prices, reviews or customer data.

## Search changes

- Explicit category selection browses actual reviewed category services in Bulgarian and English, including Other. The visible input is still filled; the internal UI flag is not an unvalidated API parameter. A specific written service, price, date, neighborhood or radius is not broadened away.
- Generic beauty browsing excludes real barbershops, even when they offer an ancillary eyebrow service. Specific eyebrow-shaping searches can still find a real published eyebrow service at a barber, shown with its actual business type.
- Appropriate GoRez partners receive convenience priority only if their actual unrounded distance from the permissioned visitor point is **at most 1 km**, and only after service, location and availability eligibility. Multiple eligible partners stay nearest-first. Unknown or more distant points receive no fabricated local priority; city-center distance is not treated as visitor location. Outside that small tier, distance remains primary globally before pagination.
- English singular/plural guitar, piano, German, English and art lesson aliases are handled deterministically. No additional model calls were introduced.
- Exact public business-name phrases, including Bulgarian transliteration, can find real reviewed catalogue records and active/public partners. Generic words and partial-word fragments do not establish an identity; expired/draft/private records remain hidden. Names never bypass explicit category, city, quarter, radius, price, duration or scheduling validation. Each source is read at most once per request, with no provider/model/budget calls; the existing server request limit remains in force. The current internal source still has its original 100-candidate read cap, which should not be confused with a guarantee to find every future partner by name.
- Two or more exact identifying words may omit a generic descriptor (e.g. “Algorithmics Питагор” for “Algorithmics · Академия Питагор”), preserving word order after removing only generic descriptors. Arbitrary missing name words, substrings and generic-only names do not qualify. Veterinary, psychiatry and tattoo vocabulary maps to the existing Other category, not invented new taxonomy or arbitrary businesses.
- Specific massage requests can match actual published massage services filed under Therapies, while explicit Other-category browsing remains category-bounded. Squash, kinesitherapy, GTP, escape-room, photo-session/studio rental and mammography vocabulary is recognised; a generic photo-studio query includes actual studio sessions, while explicit rental requests stay rental-specific. This does not invent services at other businesses.
- Structural validation is reused only for recursively immutable, already schema-validated static records. Mutable/external copies are still validated; approval/expiry is checked on every request. Service interpretation is prepared once per request for candidate filtering, not hundreds of times for the same query, without a process-global user-query cache. This is a local CPU optimisation, not a promise of zero network/database latency.
- The existing user-advanced horizontal pagination remains bounded, without an extra “More businesses” button, cloned cards, an automatic full-catalogue fetch loop or a blank tail.

## Clickable business details

Clicking a catalogue/partner photo or name opens a native modal, not a new external request. Its component is lazy-loaded only on demand. Compact cards show real services instead of the exact street address; the existing upper-left distance badge remains untouched. Exact addresses, when published, are in details and the Maps destination. Unknown points never get a substituted district/city distance.

The modal shows all active public services, actual optional prices and durations, known opening hours, the exact published address, owner site and the same Maps/booking actions. Partner data comes from the existing public service/profile/schedule data; inactive services and customer/private booking records are excluded. External booking is still a reviewed URL or phone action, not a GoRez calendar reservation or confirmed availability.

Catalogue prices/hours have strict optional schemas with source URLs and independent check dates. This snapshot includes **prices for 25 services at 15 records and opening hours at 67 records**, not full pricing/hour coverage. Missing prices are not zero/free. A “from” price is displayed as such and never establishes a maximum budget. Stale/future/unsafe-source facts disappear independently after 90 days. Missing hours are not presented as closed; unpublished weekdays are omitted, and “by appointment” remains distinct from closed. Owner-derived facts for existing records are kept in `details-enrichment-v2.mjs` without multiplying business counts or changing IDs. Conditional prices retain the actual option (e.g. short-nail strengthening or pedicure without a mask), not a misleading price for every service variant.

The modal contains keyboard focus, supports Escape/backdrop close, locks background scrolling and restores focus/body state. The mobile layout stays within the viewport and scrolls long content; both action buttons remain reachable. Source text is rendered as plain React text, never HTML.
