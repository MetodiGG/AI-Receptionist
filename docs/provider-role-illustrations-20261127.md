# Reviewed GoRez catalogue illustrations · 2026-11-27

This is an illustration-only successor to `f6cab3a9ea894b6b285dee886b549f1021400389`.
It does not change published records, providers, offers, prices, eligibility,
design, analytics, directions, bookings, calendars, or deployment settings.

- The [exact assignment manifest](../lib/provider-role-illustrations.json) is
  the source of truth for these corrections. A reviewer established the
  professional's role from the public profile and compared it with the person
  **performing** the service in the actually rendered stock image. The client's
  appearance, an address containing a personal name, an unidentified staff
  member, or a brand name on its own is not evidence.
- In the reported example, the old shop illustration showed a woman as the
  lawyer and a man as the client. The replacement shows a fictional male lawyer
  providing legal consultation. The family-law service image already showed a
  male lawyer and stays. For the reviewed woman lawyer example the opposite is
  true: the cover stays and the legal service illustration changes.
- All replacement files are local WebPs made with the built-in ImageGen tool:
  720 × 540 business covers and 480 × 480 service pictures. The brief for each
  was to show the corresponding male or female fictional actor performing the
  actual service, in an everyday editorial composition with no text, brand,
  or resemblance to a named business or individual. They are not photos of the
  named individuals or their premises. Neither genuine photographs nor
  category/subcategory images are modified. Existing AI disclosures are retained.
- Corrections apply only to a signed-off catalogue branch, a specified
  previously mismatched image path, and (for the detail menu) an exact offer
  name. If the new image fails to load, the old mismatched scene cannot return
  as the small cover derivative or a known image retry. Approved authentic
  photos continue to take priority.
- Ambiguous commercial names, team services, and scenes with no identifiable
  provider were not assigned an actor on a guess. This release does not
  automatically infer this attribute for future listings.
